Data connections

TradingView (default) needs nothing. Anonymous history: ~1 week of 1m, ~4 weeks of 5m, ~1.5 years of 1H. A TradingView sessionid cookie of your account raises the bar limits (plan dependent) and enables the “verify indicators” check.

OANDA v20 is the price feed behind TradingView's OANDA symbols. With a free practice-account API token you get years of 1m bid/ask history and 5-second candles for the exact 30-second entry and SL/TP resolution.

Saved only to the local .env file in this project (permissions 600).

TradingView history download

Pages backwards through TradingView history (like scrolling the chart left) until the date below or until your plan's limit, and keeps everything in data_cache/. Takes several minutes for 1m data.
