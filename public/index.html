MOMO trades breakouts on the 5-minute chart of EURUSD, GBPUSD, AUDUSD, USDJPY, USDCHF and EURJPY. A trade needs three signals to line up within a few candles: a candle body closing through the 20 EMA, a Supertrend flip, and a MACD histogram "hook" that turns momentum the other way.

It only trades when the hourly ADX shows a healthy trend (25–40) in the 11:00–22:00 Bulgarian-time sessions and stays out around medium and high impact news. The stop sits on the Supertrend and the target is 1.6× the stop. Prices are OANDA's own candles, with 5-second data for the entry moment.

How MOMO works and how it is tested

Entry

  • Trend filter: at 11:00 and 15:00 Bulgarian time the ADX(10) of the last hourly candle is read. Between 25 and 40 opens the session (11:00–15:00 or 15:00–22:00).
  • EMA break: the candle that crosses EMA(20) must close with its body at least 0.8 pip beyond it, and be no larger than 12 pips.
  • Supertrend(3, 1.5): the break must come on the flip candle or the next one.
  • MACD hook: the histogram changes colour after 2–5 fading bars, and keeps strengthening up to entry.
  • Entry: 30 seconds into the next candle, only if momentum is still building at that moment.

Exit and risk

  • Stop on the average Supertrend of the signal and entry candles. Target 1.6× the stop distance. 0.2 lots, one position per pair.
  • No new trades within 10 minutes of medium or high impact news; open trades close 10 minutes before it. Everything closes at 22:00 Bulgarian time.

Testing

  • OANDA mid candles (what TradingView's OANDA charts show), 1-minute bars for exits and 5-second bars for the 30-second checks.
  • No look-ahead: every check uses only data available at that moment. When SL and TP are hit in the same bar, finer data decides, else the loss is assumed.
